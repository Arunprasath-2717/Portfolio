# Surface: Home + case studies (Experience mode)

Audience: recruiters and engineers screening for SWE internships/entry roles. Job: name two projects and reach Arun in one visit. Constraint: LeetCode is link-only, no stats.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a controller's flight-strip board: every project is a printed strip racked by kind, carrying what goes in, what gets decided, and the stack. It refuses the gradient hero over three equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Matte steel-green board, buff paper strips in exactly three holder inks (amber = AI systems, cobalt = platforms, signal red = data studies), near-black print, ruled field columns, condensed heavy callsigns, mono only for strip data fields.

STORY: Visitor reads Arun's own header strip (name, role, links), scans the active rack of four flagships, opens one, sees its pipeline worked live, then follows one unbroken time rail of internships and events to a contact close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width board. Top: Arun's header strip spanning the width, name huge at left in the callsign box, role and focus in fields, GitHub/LinkedIn/LeetCode/Email as right-hand fields. Below: rack of four flagship strips, each opening its case study. Primary action: open a strip.

FORM: Flight progress strip board, candidate 3 of 7 (assigned). Raises: from drum-machine step row, one unbroken time rail; from risograph, total three-ink commitment; from ukiyo-e, the keyline grid never moves between home and case pages. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
